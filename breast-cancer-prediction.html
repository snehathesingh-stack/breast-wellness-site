When to seek care

Book a clinical check for a new lump, nipple discharge, skin dimpling, swelling, or changes that persist beyond one menstrual cycle.

Monthly self-check

Look for visible changes, then gently feel the breast and underarm area. Track what is normal for you, and report new or persistent changes.

Screening

Screening needs vary by age, personal history, and family history. Follow local medical guidance or a clinician's recommendation.
